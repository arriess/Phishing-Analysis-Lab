# Investigation 003 — Business Email Compromise Scenario

**Status:** 🟡 Planned  
**Scenario type:** Simulated / Benign Training Sample

## Objective

Investigate a simulated business email compromise (BEC) scenario focused on social engineering, identity deception, payment-request context, and SOC escalation decisions.

## Planned Analysis

- Review display name, sender domain, and reply-to
- Identify impersonation and urgency indicators
- Assess payment or credential-request language
- Review email authentication results when available
- Extract relevant sanitized IOCs
- Evaluate likely business impact
- Map supported behavior to MITRE ATT&CK
- Recommend escalation, containment, and user-verification actions
