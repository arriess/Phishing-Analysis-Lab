# Training Samples

This directory documents the sample policy for the phishing-analysis lab.

Only **simulated, benign, or deliberately constructed training emails and indicators** are used.

## Rules

- No real credentials.
- No active phishing infrastructure.
- No weaponized attachments or malicious payloads.
- No targeting of real users, organizations, or third-party systems.
- Any domain examples should use safe training or reserved examples where practical.
- Public samples must be sanitized before publication.

Raw email samples are not added unless they are fully synthetic and contain no sensitive or third-party data.
